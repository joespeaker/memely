# Copyright & DMCA Policy

Memely responds to valid DMCA notices and removes infringing content quickly.

## Takedown intake

- Public endpoint/form: `POST /dmca/requests`
- Logged data: requester identity, contact, claimed work, URL, statement, timestamp.

## Review process

1. Validate notice completeness.
2. Temporarily disable/restrict disputed asset if appropriate.
3. Record action in moderation/removal logs with timestamp.
4. Notify uploader where legally required.

## Counter-notice and restoration

Counter-notice pathways and restoration windows should follow applicable law.
