# Repeat Infringer Policy

Memely tracks repeated copyright violations and can suspend or terminate accounts.

## Signals

- Confirmed DMCA removals associated with a user.
- Moderation outcomes confirming ownership abuse.

## Enforcement ladder

1. Warning for first confirmed incident.
2. Temporary suspension for repeated incidents.
3. Permanent termination for persistent abuse.

All enforcement actions must be logged with actor, reason, and timestamp.
